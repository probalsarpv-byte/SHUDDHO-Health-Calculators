Privacy model

  • Login is optional.
  • All tools remain usable in guest/local-only mode.
  • Local saves happen before cloud sync.
  • Cloud sync only runs for authenticated users when Firebase is configured.
  • Public Food Composition and MediNutrition pages are separate public resources; SHUDDHO does not intentionally append your private profile to their URLs.
