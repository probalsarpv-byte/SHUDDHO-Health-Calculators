Safety checklist

Common awareness points

  • Some medicines interact with grapefruit, vitamin K-rich foods, minerals, caffeine or alcohol.
  • Iron, calcium, magnesium and fiber can change absorption of some medicines.
  • Timing instructions vary by medicine; one rule does not fit all.
  • Supplements can interact with prescription and OTC medicines.
Use the medicine-specific source or pharmacist advice before changing timing.
