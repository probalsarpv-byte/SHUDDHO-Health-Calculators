How it works / কীভাবে কাজ করে

Evaluates how evenly daily protein is distributed across three meals.

দৈনিক protein তিনটি meal-এর মধ্যে কতটা evenly distributed তা মূল্যায়ন করে।

Formula: Balance score from breakfast, lunch and dinner protein

Interpretation & limitations

Result ≠ diagnosis

The calculator gives a formula-based estimate. Symptoms, history, medicines, age, ethnicity and laboratory context can change meaning.

Trend matters

Repeated measurements made the same way are often more useful than one isolated number.

বাংলা ব্যাখ্যা

Result-এর পাশে status ও health meaning দেখানো হবে, যাতে শুধু সংখ্যা নয়—context-ও বোঝা যায়।

Professional context

High-risk, abnormal or symptomatic results should be interpreted by an appropriate health professional.

Educational/reference use only. Do not use this page alone to diagnose disease, stop/change medicine, or make urgent treatment decisions.

FAQ

What does the Protein Distribution Score result mean?

The result is an estimate based on Balance score from breakfast, lunch and dinner protein. The page provides contextual interpretation, but it is not a diagnosis.

Is Protein Distribution Score accurate?

Accuracy depends on the quality of the inputs and the limitations of the underlying equation. Use trends and clinical context when appropriate.

Can I use this calculator in Bangla?

Yes. SHUDDHO Health Calculators supports Bangla and English on the same page.
