How V5.2 sync works

  • Every save remains local-first.
  • When signed in and online, changed SHUDDHO local entries sync automatically.
  • When another device has a newer copy, the newer timestamp wins.
  • Offline use continues; sync resumes after internet returns.
  • Local-only mode remains available permanently.
